How this calculator works

The estimate follows the standard volume method used by landscape suppliers:

volume = length × width × (depth ÷ 12) [ft³] cubic yards = volume ÷ 27 gravel needed = volume × (1 + compaction extra) tons = cubic yards × density (washed stone ≈ 1.4 tons/yd³) bags = gravel needed ÷ 0.5 [0.5 cu ft per bag]
volume = length × width × (depth ÷ 100) [m³] gravel needed = volume × (1 + compaction extra) tons = volume × 1.5 (washed stone ≈ 1.5 t/m³)
⚠️ Estimates only. Gravel density varies by quarry (1.3–1.6 tons per cubic yard) and depth varies with subgrade. Round up when ordering, and confirm quantities with your supplier.

Choosing the right depth

  • Decorative coverage: 2 inches is enough to cover landscape fabric around beds and borders.
  • Walkways & paths: 3 inches, laid over compacted base.
  • Driveways: 4 inches minimum, 6–8 inches in two lifts for vehicles or soft ground.
  • French drain backfill: the trench is what matters — use the french drain calculator instead, since pipe volume and trench walls change the math.
  • Call before you dig. In the US dial 811 to have buried utilities marked — it's free and it's the law.

Frequently asked questions

How many square feet does a ton of gravel cover?

At the recommended 2-inch depth for decorative coverage, one ton of gravel covers roughly 100 to 120 square feet. At a 4-inch depth (driveways and french drains) one ton covers about 50 to 60 square feet. This calculator does the exact math for your area.

How deep should gravel be for a driveway?

A residential gravel driveway needs at least 4 inches of compacted gravel, and 6 to 8 inches is better for vehicles or soft soil, usually laid in two lifts. Multiply the area by the depth with this calculator and add 10 to 20 percent for compaction.

How much does a cubic yard of gravel weigh?

A cubic yard of typical gravel weighs about 1.4 tons (2,800 pounds). Washed stone runs 1.3 to 1.5 tons per cubic yard depending on quarry and moisture, which is why this calculator lets you pick the material type.

How many 0.5 cubic feet bags of gravel do I need?

One cubic yard equals 27 cubic feet, so a cubic yard is 54 half-cubic-foot bags. Bags make sense for small jobs under about a cubic yard; beyond that, ordering by the cubic yard or ton from a landscape supplier is far cheaper.

Should I add extra gravel for compaction?

Yes. Gravel compacts and settles, so add 10 to 20 percent for driveways and base layers, and about 30 percent when backfilling a french drain trench. This calculator already includes the compaction allowance you choose.
