Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

DrainCalc (tools.slidevaulte.com) provides free drainage and landscaping calculators. This policy explains what information we collect and how it is used. In short: our calculators run entirely in your browser, and we do not collect, store, or share any numbers you enter.

Calculator inputs

All calculations — trench dimensions, roof area, runoff figures, and every other value you type — are processed locally in your web browser. Nothing you enter is sent to, stored on, or visible to our servers. Closing the page erases everything.

Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to understand overall site traffic (page views, referring sites, approximate country, device type). Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, does not fingerprint individual users, and does not collect personal data. It complies with GDPR, CCPA, and PECR.

Advertising

If we display advertising in the future (for example, Google AdSense), third-party vendors including Google may use cookies to serve ads based on prior visits to this or other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. This section will be updated before any ads go live.

Affiliate links

Some pages may contain affiliate links to retailers. If you click one and make a purchase, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. Affiliate clicks are tracked by the retailer's own systems under their privacy policies.

Data we do not collect

  • No accounts or sign-ups — we never ask for your name or email to use a calculator
  • No calculator inputs or results
  • No advertising or tracking cookies of our own
  • No selling of any user data — there is no user data to sell

Children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13.

Your rights

Depending on your location (e.g. EU/EEA, California), you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since we do not collect personal data, there is generally nothing to access or delete. If you have any privacy question, contact us at the address on our Contact page.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top reflects the current version.
