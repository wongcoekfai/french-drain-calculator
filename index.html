How this calculator works

A french drain is a gravel-filled trench with a perforated pipe that redirects surface and groundwater away from an area. The material estimate follows standard excavation practice:

trench volume = length × (width ÷ 12) × (depth ÷ 12) [ft³] pipe volume = π × (diameter ÷ 24)² × length [ft³] gravel needed = (trench volume − pipe volume) × 1.30 [30% extra for compaction & spillage] cubic yards = gravel ÷ 27 tons = cubic yards × 1.4 (washed stone ≈ 1.4 tons/yd³) fabric area = length × (2 × depth + width) × 1.15 [15% overlap, lines both walls + bottom]
trench volume = length × (width ÷ 100) × (depth ÷ 100) [m³] pipe volume = π × (diameter ÷ 200)² × length [m³] gravel needed = (trench volume − pipe volume) × 1.30 tons = cubic meters × 1.5 (washed stone ≈ 1.5 t/m³) fabric area = length × (2 × depth + width) × 1.15 [m², with 15% overlap]
⚠️ Estimates only. Gravel density varies by quarry (1.3–1.5 tons per cubic yard), and every trench has irregularities. Round up when ordering, and confirm quantities with your supplier.

Planning tips

  • Slope it. Keep a 1% grade (1 inch drop per 8–10 ft) along the whole run — water needs somewhere to go.
  • Dig wider, not deeper. A 12-inch trench with 4-inch pipe leaves a healthy gravel column on both sides of the pipe.
  • Washed gravel only. 0.5–1.5 in washed stone; fines and sand will clog the system within a few seasons.
  • Wrap it. Non-woven geotextile fabric (or a pipe sock) is what keeps your drain working in year ten instead of year two.
  • Call before you dig. In the US dial 811 to have buried utilities marked — it's free and it's the law.

Frequently asked questions

How deep should a french drain be?

Most residential french drains are dug 18 to 24 inches deep and 8 to 12 inches wide. The trench must sit below the problem area and slope continuously toward the outlet at about 1% (1 inch of drop per 8–10 feet).

How much gravel do I need for a french drain?

Gravel volume equals the trench volume minus the volume taken up by the pipe, plus roughly 30% extra for compaction and spillage. This calculator does that math for you and reports the result in cubic yards and tons.

What size gravel is best for a french drain?

Washed gravel between 0.5 and 1.5 inches is the standard choice. Washed stone packs well and lets water pass through freely; avoid crushed fines or pea gravel mixed with sand, which clog over time.

Do I need filter fabric in a french drain?

Yes. Line the trench with non-woven geotextile fabric before adding gravel, or use perforated pipe with a fabric sock. Fabric prevents soil from migrating into the gravel and clogging the drain, and is the single biggest factor in how long the drain lasts.

What slope should a french drain have?

Aim for a 1% grade — about 1 inch of fall for every 8 to 10 feet of run. Less than that and water sits in the pipe; much more and water rushes past, which can scour the trench.
