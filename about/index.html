About DrainCalc

Free, transparent calculators for drainage, grading and landscaping projects.

Why we built this

Most drainage and landscaping questions that come up mid-project — how many yards of gravel for a 40-foot trench?, how big should my dry well be?, what slope does this pipe need? — are simple math, buried under forum threads, videos and calculators hidden behind sign-up walls.

DrainCalc does the opposite: instant results, no account, no paywall, and the actual formula shown on every page so you can check our work.

How the calculators work

Every calculator runs entirely in your browser. We use standard civil-engineering and landscaping formulas — trench volume minus pipe displacement, the Rational Method for storm runoff (Q = C×i×A), the 1%-grade rule for drainage pipe, standard gravel and mulch densities — and we publish each formula right next to its calculator, including the safety margins (like the 30% gravel overage or 15% fabric overlap) so nothing is hidden.

Results are planning estimates. Real-world materials vary by quarry, region and site conditions, so always round up and confirm quantities with your supplier.

Who it's for

  • Homeowners planning a weekend french drain, dry well or regrading project
  • DIYers estimating bags vs. bulk deliveries before heading to the store
  • Contractors and landscapers who need quick, defensible numbers for quotes

Is it really free?

Yes. The site is supported by unobtrusive advertising and affiliate links (for example, links to tools and materials you might already be about to buy). That's the whole business model — you never pay, and we never ask for your email.
